About Circle: Our Story and Vision

Our Beginnings

Founded in 2024 by two friends with a shared passion for photography and building a community, Circle has evolved from casual meetups to a thriving community of over 700 members from diverse backgrounds and skill levels.

Our Philosophy

We believe photography is about connection—with places, moments, and each other. Our theme represents our inclusive approach, embracing diverse perspectives and creative expressions that make our community vibrant.

FAQs

How do I join the next event?

Simply sign up through the Meetup page or Instagram link. First-time participants receive a special Lightroom preset and an invitation to our official LINE group!

Do I need professional equipment?

Not at all! Many members use smartphones with creative apps. We focus on composition and vision rather than gear. We have equipment lending options for special workshops.

Are there membership fees?

Membership is free! Some of the photowalks are paid which only covers for the extrance fees for the venues.
