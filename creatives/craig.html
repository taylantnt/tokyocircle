Craig Stennett

Portrait & Street Photographer
Exploring the hidden beauty of Japan's landscapes and cultural heritage through the lens has been my passion for over a decade. My name is Craig, and I am a professional photographer who has been living in Japan for more than 10 years. Being multilingual and the co-founder of Circle, I enjoy meeting new people and collaborating on creative projects. Recently, I have been experimenting with the shutter drag technique to capture the serene moments and breathtaking vistas that make Japan unique, from misty mountain temples to modern cityscapes at twilight.
