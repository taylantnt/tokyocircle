Our Happy Little Photo Family

No experience needed - just bring your smile and creativity!

"Circle" connects creatives

We help photographers, models, hair and makeup artists and other creatives to connect and collaborate.

Founded in 2024 by two friends, Circle has evolved from casual meetups to a thriving community. We emerged as a circle of friends passionate about photography, modeling and art.

Circle offers opportunities to meet like-minded creatives and create in a fun environment. Our photowalks take place in different parts of Tokyo, as well as weekend trips outside the city.

All skill levels are welcome! Come join our circle of artists and friends.
